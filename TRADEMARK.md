The source code of the **DOOMSCRLL** ecosystem is made available under the open-source [Apache License, Version 2.0](LICENSE).

However, **Apache License 2.0 Section 6 explicitly excludes trademark rights**. This document outlines the official policy regarding the use of the **DOOMSCRLL** name, brand assets, logos, and visual design identity.

The following intellectual property assets remain strictly reserved and owned by the DOOMSCRLL project creators:

- **Brand Names & Marks**: "DOOMSCRLL", "DOOMLIT", "DOOMSCRLL App", "DOOMSCRLL Webapp".
- **Logos & Graphics**: Official DOOMSCRLL logo, icon marks, ticket mask graphics, typography, and brand assets stored under `Brand Assets/` or `public/` directories.

## 3. Permitted Uses (No Explicit Permission Required)

You may use the DOOMSCRLL name and brand assets without separate written permission for:

- **Educational & Personal Use**: Running local developer builds, inspecting the codebase, or studying the architecture for personal education.
- **Attribution & Reference**: Referencing DOOMSCRLL in blog posts, news articles, academic research, or portfolio reviews (e.g., _"Built as a fork of DOOMSCRLL"_ or _"Interoperable with DOOMSCRLL"_).
- **Linking**: Adding standard hyperlinks to official DOOMSCRLL web properties ([https://doomscrll.com](https://doomscrll.com)).
- **Pull Requests & Code Contributions**: Contributing fixes or improvements to the official DOOMSCRLL repositories.

## 4. Prohibited Uses & Rebranding Requirement for Forks

If you create a public fork, derivative work, or commercial product based on the DOOMSCRLL codebase:

- **Rebranding Required**: You **MUST REBRAND** your fork or distribution. You must remove all official DOOMSCRLL logos, icon assets, and replace references to "DOOMSCRLL" or "DOOMLIT" with your own distinct project name and branding.
- **No Impression of Affiliation**: You may not use the DOOMSCRLL name, logo, or brand assets in a manner that implies endorsement, sponsorship, or official affiliation with the original DOOMSCRLL project.
- **No Commercial Use of Brand**: You may not sell products, services, or subscriptions using the DOOMSCRLL name or trademark.
